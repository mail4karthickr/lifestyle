# Photo credits

The hotels in this POC are fictional. Their photos are real photographs of other hotels from
[Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org), used under the free licenses below. They are
resized and cropped; nothing else is changed. The photographers and the hotels shown are not
connected with this project and do not endorse it.

The photos are in `TravelAndLifeStyle/Assets.xcassets/Lifestyle/Photos/`; which hotel shows which
one is set in `Lifestyle/Hotel/HotelCatalog.swift`. Replace them with the hotel supplier's own
photos before this ships.
